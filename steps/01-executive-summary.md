# Step 1: Executive summary

**Reads:** `features/<slug>/brief.md` for the first pass, then every other approved step for the final pass.

Write it first in five lines, so every person and AI knows what you're building and why now. Rewrite it last, once the other steps are approved.

## First pass (before Step 2)

- [ ] What is it, and who is it for?
- [ ] Why now?
- [ ] What is the value to users, and to the business?
- [ ] What do we already know is risky or unknown?
- [ ] What decision is being asked of the reader?

Five lines. A person approves this first pass before Step 2 starts, so every later step works from the same frame.

## Final pass (after Step 9)

- [ ] Rewrite the five lines from the approved steps. Change anything the later steps proved wrong.
- [ ] What is the top delivery risk?
- [ ] How ready is it to build: green, amber or red, and why?
- [ ] Which open questions still block it, and who owns each?

## Output

Short enough to read in 30 seconds. One paragraph, then the readiness colour and the decision requested.

## Every step also

- List sources and cite entry IDs.
- Label each assumption with a confidence and what would disprove it.
- Apply the rules that bind this area. Reflect earlier decisions.
- Never present an assumption as fact.
- The step may be marked "not needed", with a reason.
