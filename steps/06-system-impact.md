# Step 6: System impact

**Reads:** Step 3 and Step 5 (approved).

## Think through

- [ ] A simple diagram of the affected modules and how they connect.
- [ ] For every module the team has named: touched or not, how, dependency, risk and confidence.

## Watch for

- Do not invent module names. Use only the ones the team has given you. Ask if the list is missing.
