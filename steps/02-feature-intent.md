# Step 2: Feature intent

**Reads:** `features/<slug>/brief.md`, Step 1 (approved first pass) and in-scope approved entries. This is the first full step.

## Think through

- [ ] What is the user problem?
- [ ] Who are the primary and secondary users?
- [ ] What is the pain today, and what is the workaround?
- [ ] What outcome do we want?
- [ ] What is in scope, and what is out?
- [ ] Why now?
- [ ] Which statements are facts (with a source) and which are inferences (with a confidence)?
- [ ] Epic: goal, acceptance criteria, definition of done.
- [ ] Success measures: each with a target, a timeframe and a baseline. "Unknown" is a valid baseline.

## Watch for

- Do not write acceptance criteria for areas still undecided. Raise a question instead.

## Every step also

- List sources and cite entry IDs.
- Label each assumption with a confidence and what would disprove it.
- Apply the rules that bind this area. Reflect earlier decisions.
- The step may be marked "not needed", with a reason.
