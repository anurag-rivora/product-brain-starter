# Step 9: Delivery backlog

**Reads:** Step 2, the specs (Steps 4, 5 and 7) and the plan (Step 8), all approved. The backlog is the last new step. Then rewrite the summary in Step 1.

## Think through

- [ ] Stories from the end user's view: "As a ..., I want ..., so that ...".
- [ ] Slice by what the user can see or do.
- [ ] Enabler stories for invisible platform work, each tied to what it unblocks.
- [ ] Each story has 2 to 5 Given/When/Then criteria.
- [ ] A definition of done.
- [ ] Priority.
- [ ] Owning discipline, as a tag (for example frontend or backend). Never as the persona.
- [ ] Dependencies, and the step each story came from.
- [ ] The number of stories follows the complexity. Do not pad.

## Watch for

- Nothing goes to your tracker until a person says so.

## Every step also

- List sources and cite entry IDs.
- Label each assumption with a confidence and what would disprove it.
- Apply the rules that bind this area. Reflect earlier decisions.
- The step may be marked "not needed", with a reason.
