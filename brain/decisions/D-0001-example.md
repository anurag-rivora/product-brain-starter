# Customers on the legacy plan keep paper invoices until June 2027

**Decision.** Customers on the legacy plan keep receiving paper invoices until 30 June 2027. After that date, invoices are sent by email only.

**Why.** About a fifth of legacy-plan customers have no email on file, and the notice period in their agreements runs to June 2027.

**Source line.** "We can't switch them off before the notice period ends." (sources/2026-11-12-billing-sync.md)

**Consequences.** Any change to invoice delivery must keep the paper path for this group until the date above.
